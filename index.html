About SlotSync

The problem

Universities, labs and hostels share expensive, limited things: lab equipment, study rooms, one washing machine, one sports court. Today that is managed with a paper sign-up sheet taped to a wall, or by shouting into a WhatsApp group.

  • Two people write on the same line — a double-booking.
  • Nobody can see what is free without walking over to look.
  • Someone books and never turns up, and the slot is wasted.
  • Arguments get settled by whoever is loudest, not by the schedule.

Who it helps

  • Students — see what is free in seconds and claim it.
  • Lab and hostel staff — stop being the human booking desk.
  • Everyone — a written record instead of a memory argument.

It is built for phones first, works offline, and needs no login and no server.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. 1
    Pick a resource. Tap a card at the top. The week below changes to that resource.
  2. 2
    Tap a free slot. A form opens with the date and time already filled in. Add your name and purpose, then confirm.
  3. 3
    Manage it later. Tap your booking to edit or cancel it, or use “My bookings” to find everything under your name.

If a slot is taken, SlotSync tells you exactly what it clashes with and offers the nearest free times instead.
